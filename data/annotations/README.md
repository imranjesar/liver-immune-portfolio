# Versioned public gene sets

`mh.all.v2026.2.Mm.symbols.gmt` is the unchanged mouse MSigDB MH Hallmark collection (50 orthology-mapped gene sets), downloaded from the Broad Institute release archive. Exact URL and SHA256 are recorded in `metadata/hallmark_source.json`.

Copyright Broad Institute, MIT and Regents of the University of California. MSigDB v2026.2 data are provided under [CC BY 4.0 with MSigDB terms](https://gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb_license_terms.jsp). This collection is MH, not a KEGG or BioCarta collection. Credit MSigDB and the original Hallmark publication when reusing these data. No changes were made to gene-set membership in the distributed GMT; analytic intersection/filtering is recorded separately.
