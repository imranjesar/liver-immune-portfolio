# Figure captions

All figures are an independent reanalysis of public mouse liver CD45+ immune-cell data from [GSE338157](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE338157). GEO describes three sample-tagged mice per condition: fibrosis, ACLF plus vehicle and ACLF plus CCS1477. Each condition has one multiplexed capture library. **Condition and capture library are confounded; the figures describe exploratory associations and do not establish a capture-adjusted or causal drug effect.**

## Figure 1. A provisional immune-cell atlas after QC and doublet sensitivity analysis

**A**, UMAP of 44,188 retained cells from nine mouse tags after a 500-6,000 detected-gene / ≤20% mitochondrial threshold and exclusion of 2,766 cells predicted as doublets in all three residual-rate scenarios. Seurat normalization, 2,000 variable genes, 20 principal components and resolution 0.4 were used for the separate retained-cell clustering. Numeric callouts identify the 13 provisional broad populations listed in panel B; colors denote these populations. Drawing order was randomized. Unresolved and rate-sensitive cells remain visible in the atlas and are not automatically eligible for pseudobulk analysis. **B**, canonical lineage-marker expression. Dot size indicates the percentage of cells with at least one raw UMI; fill indicates the mean log-normalized expression standardized within each gene across the displayed populations. Color limits are capped at z = -1.5 and 1.5; uncapped values are supplied in source data. Mixed-lineage and low-complexity groups remain unresolved. Marker agreement is not proof that every barcode is a singlet. ASC, antibody-secreting cell; cDC, conventional dendritic cell-like; pDC, plasmacytoid dendritic cell; ILC, innate lymphoid cell-like.

## Figure 2. Mouse-level expression structure and illustrative interferon-associated genes

**A**, PCA of the 500 most variable blind-VST genes in each pseudobulk population. Each point represents one mouse tag, with n = 3 vehicle and n = 3 CCS1477 mice. Labels 04-06 denote vehicle tags and 10-12 denote CCS1477 tags. Axis ranges vary by population; variance fractions are shown above each panel. Fibrosis mice are not included in this contrast. **B**, unshrunk DESeq2 log2 fold changes for six data-selected interferon-leading-edge genes; positive values indicate higher modeled expression in CCS1477. Bars show pointwise, unadjusted 95% Wald intervals (estimate ±1.96 SE). Filled points indicate BH-adjusted q < 0.05 across all tested gene-population pairs in the primary contrast; open points indicate q ≥ 0.05. The six genes were selected by the smallest median global q among their significant populations, restricted to genes associated in at least three populations and present in the primary interferon-alpha/gamma leading edge in at least three populations, after display-only exclusion of mitochondrial, operational ribosomal, immunoglobulin-variable and Gm-prefixed symbols. This panel is an illustration from the same data, not an independent validation or a prespecified target panel.

Raw UMIs were summed by mouse and population. Inclusion required high-confidence lineage, no unresolved review hold, no rate-sensitive doublet call and no strong same-barcode B/T marker conflict. Every tested population required at least 20 selected cells and 10,000 UMIs in each of the six comparison mice. Genes required ≥10 counts in at least three mice. DESeq2 1.48.2 used `~ condition`, positive-count size factors, Wald tests and no automatic count replacement. The reviewed neutrophil subset and basophils did not meet the coverage threshold and were not tested. Six leave-one-mouse-out fits per population are supplied as sensitivity data; these do not resolve library confounding.

## Figure 3. Hallmark enrichment and rank-feature sensitivity

**A**, normalized enrichment scores (NES) for all 26 MSigDB Hallmark gene sets with across-population q < 0.05 in at least one primary population. Red indicates enrichment toward higher CCS1477-associated Wald statistics; blue indicates enrichment toward vehicle. An open dot denotes primary q < 0.05; a filled dot additionally requires q < 0.05 and concordant NES direction after the rank-feature sensitivity filter. The displayed gene-set names describe signatures, not diagnoses: for example, the “allograft rejection” set does not imply transplantation in this experiment. **B**, primary versus feature-filtered NES for every common tested gene-set/population pair. Filled points meet the concordant two-variant threshold; open points do not. The diagonal indicates equal NES. **C**, running enrichment curve for the primary gene-set/population pair with the smallest global q: the interferon-gamma response in CD8 T cells. Ticks mark gene-set members along the signed-Wald ranking; dashed horizontal lines indicate the maximum and minimum running enrichment score. The example was selected from the same analysis and is not an additional test.

Mouse MSigDB MH v2026.2.Mm contains 50 orthology-mapped Hallmark gene sets. fgsea 1.34.2 multilevel analysis used finite, converged DESeq2 Wald statistics, gene-set overlap sizes 15-500, `eps = 0`, 10,000 simple permutations for initialization, and a fixed seed. BH adjustment was performed across populations separately for each rank variant. The sensitivity variant removed `^mt-`, `^Rp[sl][0-9]`, `^Ig[hkl]v` symbols and Gm42418 from the rankings; the DE models were not refitted. Sixty-three of 64 primary associated gene-set/population pairs retain concordant direction and q < 0.05 under this filter. This is a robustness check against selected features, not batch correction or independent replication. Tied Wald scores occurred in approximately 0.01% of two rankings; the fixed ordering/seed is recorded, and no artificial jitter was added.

## Supplementary Figure S1. Doublet-rate and technical-feature audits

**A**, predicted doublet fractions from separate models for the three capture libraries under assumed residual rates of 2.5%, 5% and 10%, after sample-tag filtering. These rates are sensitivity assumptions rather than measured loading rates. Model thresholds need not produce the assumed percentage of calls; exclusion in the retained-cell branch required calls in all three scenarios. **B-D**, raw pseudobulk UMI fractions for mitochondrial features, an operational ribosomal-protein symbol set (`^Rp[sl][0-9]`, not an exhaustive ribosomal annotation), and Gm42418, respectively. Each point is one mouse within a reviewed population (n = 3 per condition). The same conservative pseudobulk selection used in Figure 2 applies. Gm42418 is retained as a feature-risk flag, not assigned a definitive function here. Total mitochondrial fractions do not increase uniformly across populations despite the mt-Nd4l association, underscoring the need to distinguish individual-gene, cell-state and technical effects.

## Bulk Figure 1. Liver neutrophil expression at 24 and 72 hours after injury

(A) PCA of blinded variance-stabilized counts from the 500 most variable retained features. Orange: liver 24 h (four libraries); blue: liver 72 h (five libraries). Numbers identify replicate labels within each group, linked to GSM accessions in `config/bulk_samples.tsv`. PC1 and PC2 explain 51.1% and 26.4% of variance. Every liver library is retained; mouse provenance/pooling per library is not independently established from retrieved metadata. (B) Unshrunk DESeq2 log2 fold changes (72 h / 24 h) against log10(mean normalized count +1), for 7,327 features. Colored points meet gene-level BH q<0.05: 364 higher and 638 lower at 72 h; other features, including missing adjusted values, are gray. Point size/opacity do not encode significance. (C) Summed supplied gene counts for all 12 libraries, including three spleen controls excluded from inference. These are processed-count totals rather than sequencing read depth. (D) Ten Hallmark sets with smallest primary BH q, ordered by NES. Point size shows -log10(q), color the enrichment direction. **None of the 45 eligible primary sets meets q<0.05** (minimum q=0.148); the panel is descriptive, not a list of significant pathways. All nine liver libraries enter DESeq2 `~condition`; counts >=10 in at least three libraries define the fixed feature universe. Hallmark ranking uses signed Wald statistics and an explicit unambiguous-symbol policy, with fgseaMultilevel and BH across eligible sets. See `docs/BULK_ANALYSIS.md` for mapping, model and rank details. Source: GSE247373, Maali et al. (2024), doi:10.1097/HC9.0000000000000459; original data generation is credited to those investigators.

## Bulk Supplementary Figure S1. Library and rank sensitivity

(A) Primary log2 fold change versus the minimum across nine leave-one-library-out DESeq2 fits, for the 1,002 primary features with BH q<0.05. All retained primary direction and converged in every omission fit. The diagonal indicates equality; the minimum is not an uncertainty interval. Removing each of nine libraries leaves three or four 24 h libraries and four or five 72 h libraries. Each fit uses the fixed primary gene universe. No omission fit is treated as a new discovery analysis. (B) Primary Hallmark NES versus NES after removing mitochondrial, operational ribosomal, immunoglobulin-variable and Gm42418 rank features. Open points denote pairs failing the both-variants q<0.05/same-direction criterion; all pairs are open because neither variant yields a significant set. This rank sensitivity does not refit the DE model or resolve unrecorded experimental batch. All 45 sets eligible in both variants are shown. The GMT version, attribution and license are the same as for the single-cell figures above.
